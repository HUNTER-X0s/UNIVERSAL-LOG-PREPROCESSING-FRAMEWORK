## 3. Subsystem Fault Isolation Guarantees

1. **Decoupled Ingestion:** Downstream failures in AI Copilot, Threat Intelligence matching, or Graph correlation do not impede the raw event intake thread or cause packet dropping at the perimeter.
2. **Deterministic Stage Errors:** Any error during multi-stage processing is recorded in `stage_errors: dict[str, str]` attached to the pipeline result, allowing security analysts to inspect anomalies without losing telemetry.
3. **Fail-Closed Security Boundaries:** Authentication, authorization, and cryptographic signature checks fail closed immediately upon fault or malformed tokens.
